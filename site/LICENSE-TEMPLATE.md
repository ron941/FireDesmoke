styles in `dist/vendor/vggt-template.css` are adapted from the
[VGGT project website](https://github.com/vgg-t/vgg-t.github.io), commit

Changes replace the research content and resources with FireDesmoke, retain local
fonts, add accessible image comparisons and result tables, and omit upstream
analytics, remote scripts, video and 3D viewers. The original researchers do
not endorse this adaptation.

`dist/vendor/bulma.min.css` is the unchanged Bulma 0.9.1 stylesheet distributed
by that template; it retains its MIT notice and accompanying
`dist/vendor/BULMA-LICENSE.txt`. Locally served fonts have separate OFL notices.

These notices concern the website template and its dependencies. FireDesmoke
model/training/evaluation code, weights, datasets and manuscript figures are
separate material; their presence in this repository does not
announce a public distribution license. Selected figure provenance is in
`../registry/site_asset_provenance.json`.
